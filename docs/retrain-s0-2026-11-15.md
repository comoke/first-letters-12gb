Trainer: the winner's `train_cloud_hn.py`, unchanged, inside `fl-claim-a7:final`, with the training packs from hf-data (sha256 checked).

## Run facts

- **BN under block checkpointing** (bn_check.py, one step on identical data):
  - With the trainer's restore, BN buffers equal the no-checkpoint run exactly (diff 0.0), and BN updates 4× per step.
  - Without the restore, BN updates 8× per step and drifts by up to 0.20 (mean) and 2.7 (var).
  - The no-checkpoint run doesn't fit in 12 GB at micro 4, so this check ran at micro 2.
- **Train:** 3,058 s (50.4 min) for 600 steps.
  - Peak `max_memory_allocated` 8.56 GB; whole-card peak 10,655 MiB (nvidia-smi, desktop included).
  - Checkpoint sha256 `b47254aa…` (the shipped one is `9a281284…`; GPU kernels differ bit for bit).
- **Eval:** the exact shipped member recipe on the PHerc0306B claim render: roi 115 4166 694 7638, grid origin 115 694, clip_to_roi, TTA 1, nsig 3, stride 21. 56 min.

## Result

| pair | r | mean abs diff | max abs diff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **retrained s0 vs shipped s0** | **0.99958** | 0.0025 | 0.064 |
| shipped s0 vs s1 | 0.93238 | 0.0357 | 0.498 |
| shipped s0 vs s2 | 0.93665 | 0.0348 | 0.613 |
| shipped s1 vs s2 | 0.94223 | 0.0425 | 0.507 |
| retrained s0 vs shipped s1 / s2 | 0.9314 / 0.9365 | 0.036 / 0.035 | 0.52 / 0.61 |
| our replay of the shipped s0 checkpoint vs shipped s0 (repro floor) | 1.00000 | 5e-5 | 7.4e-4 |

**Verdict:** the retrain falls within the seed spread, and is far tighter than it. Retrained s0 is about as close to shipped s0 (r 0.9996) as the seed variation allows, and its distance to s1 and s2 matches the shipped s0's own. The same seed and data stream on a 12 GB card, with block checkpointing, reproduces the training run to within float non-determinism.

## Host notes

On this PC, WSL charges VRAM to Windows commit (~8.7 GB per 8 GiB). The run needs the C: pagefile to grow (8 to 16 GB), and it ran under a host watchdog (`pc/retrain/retrain_chain.sh`).
