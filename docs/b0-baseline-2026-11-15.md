# B0 baseline, 2026-11-15

**B0** is our name for a fixed test window of the PHerc0306B claim render around the claim box (about 5.85 cm²) and the baseline maps on it. **H1** and **H2** are two follow-up ideas for scans at another resolution, not part of this entry: H1 rescales the input to the model's 8.64 µm, H2 re-estimates BatchNorm statistics on the target render (AdaBN).

Inputs: windows of the PHerc0306B claim render (`pc/b0/b0_make.py`). The native variant runs the full render inside a window on the shipped tiling: TRACE crop 1516–2916 × 2038–7639; A-hn `--roi`, `--grid-origin 115 694`, clip_to_roi. In the claim box it reproduces the shipped maps: TRACE bit-identical; A-hn members r 0.9999999, max diff 6e-4.

Pipeline: TRACE v3B × A-hn (s0, s1, s2) plus the decoy member, at `--vram` 4.5–5. These low-VRAM settings give the same maps as the shipped settings.

## What it says

- **Resampling alone changes the map little.** Resampling the claim area to 9.36 µm keeps AP 0.957, AUC 0.997 and r 0.979 against the shipped map.
  - This is agreement with the winner's own map on the same surface, not accuracy against labels. It does not show that the model reads a scroll scanned natively at that resolution.
  - What `deg936` does not model: noise, contrast, energy (keV), and the papyrus itself.
  - On native scans, see villa PR [#2512](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/pull/2512) (DeepakEz; open as of 2026-11-18). Models trained only on renders pooled from 2.4 µm scans score a mean ROC-AUC of 0.74–0.78 over three seeds (0.78–0.80 with mirror TTA) against labels on PHerc 0139's five native 9.362 µm segments, a scroll held out of training (author-reported, open PR). A pooled-to-native kernel raises the mean by +0.024 (+0.011 with TTA), and self-training on the target scroll by +0.035 (+0.018). That test differs from `deg936` in model, scroll, labels and scanner, so the numbers are not comparable. It is a direct measurement of accuracy on native scans, though for the public flat recipe, which the PR itself says stronger readers beat; it does not measure the winner's models.
  - H1's room on B0 is small (0.957 → 1.0). H1 and H2 should next target a degradation that includes noise.
- **The prod-vs-prodd decoy Jaccard is the wrong false-positive metric.** Real text scores 0.82 and the controls 0.44, because both maps are TRACE × something, so the overlap measures TRACE dominance.
  - The TRACE-free check (A-hn mean vs decoy member) is low everywhere, 0.06–0.16.
- **Cross-family agreement separates text from controls:** TRACE ~ A-hn mean r is 0.75 on text (native and 9.36 µm), 0.44 on no-text, and 0.14 on shuffled depth.
  - We use it as B0's main false-positive metric, with ensA-vs-decoy J as the decoy metric.
- **Controls put zero pixels above the native top-1% threshold** (shuf and notext).
- **Throughput:** 5.1–5.4 cm² per GPU-hour for the full product plus the decoy (TRACE about 5 min, each member about 15 min per 5.85 cm²).
  - deg936 was slower (22 min per member on fewer pixels). Unexplained so far; it runs on a crop zarr without an ROI, and there may have been contention.
  - notext's stage times include a halted pass.

## Caveats

- **No ground truth.** The letter metric is agreement with the shipped map. notext is chosen by the shipped map, so it is descriptive.
- **The degradation is resampling only,** so `deg936` measures how much the map changes with resolution, not how well the model reads a native scan (see the PR #2512 note above).
- **Stage times are wall time,** not GPU-active time.
